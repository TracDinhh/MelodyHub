# MelodyHub

MelodyHub is a web music application for browsing and managing music catalog content.

## Language

**Song**:
A track in the MelodyHub music catalog. A song may belong to an album or exist as a standalone single.
_Avoid_: Track record, audio row

**Published Song**:
A song that is visible in normal listener-facing catalog views.
_Avoid_: Approved song, active song

**Song Lyric Line**:
A timed line of lyrics attached to a song.
_Avoid_: Lyrics row, subtitle row

**Album**:
A release container that can group songs for one artist.
_Avoid_: Collection, record

**Artist**:
A performer connected to songs and albums.
_Avoid_: Singer, creator
